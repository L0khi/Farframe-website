ABOUT FARFRAME

Industrial intelligence designed for the real operation.

Farframe Inc. is an industrial automation and intelligent-systems company developing software, computer-vision, data, integration, and future physical-system capabilities for operations-intensive organizations.

OPERATING POSITION

Build from the problem outward.

Farframe focuses on operational failures that sit between digital information and physical activity: documents that remain unstructured, materials that become difficult to locate, systems that do not reflect the floor, and processes that require better visibility or control.

Problem-led engineering

Begin with the operational objective and constraints before selecting the technology.

Designed for existing operations

Work with established people, systems, and infrastructure wherever practical.

Clear human accountability

Keep outputs understandable, reviewable, and connected to responsible decisions.

Modular expansion

Allow a focused system to evolve into broader connected automation over time.

CAPABILITY FRAME

One engineering direction across software and physical systems.

Farframe’s product and research direction connects document intelligence, operational data, computer vision, integration, automation, and intelligent machines through a common focus on visibility, control, and measurable operational usefulness.

01 / DATA

Structured operational information

Convert fragmented records and documents into reliable, usable data.

02 / VISION

Perception of physical activity

Use computer vision to improve awareness of materials, assets, and events.

03 / CONTROL

Connected workflows and systems

Integrate information, software, sensors, and established operational tools.

04 / AUTONOMY

Future intelligent physical systems

Extend the same foundations into robotics and autonomous operational capability.

ENGINEERED FOR IMPACT

Farframe reviews qualified enquiries involving document intelligence, material visibility, computer vision, integration, automation, and purpose-built intelligent systems.
