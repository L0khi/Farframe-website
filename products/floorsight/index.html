FloorSight

A clearer starting point for finding materials.

PROPOSED SYSTEM / CONCEPT STAGE

Proposed architecture & pilot

A proposed RFID-assisted search workflow for parts, pallets and material containers.

Search a part number, check its last detected zone and use a forklift-mounted or handheld reader to help identify the selected tagged material nearby. The goal is less search time and unnecessary travel.

A focused first pilot

One warehouse area, representative materials and one reader configuration. Establish baseline search times before evaluating improvement.

What to measure

Search time, detection reliability, correct material identification and operator usability.

What remains to validate

Tag placement, performance around metal, reader coverage and zone assignment. Last detected location does not confirm current position.

FloorSight is a proposed design, not a demonstrated hardware system. Continuous warehouse-wide tracking and exact positioning are not established capabilities.
