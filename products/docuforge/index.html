Read the demonstration summary

Invoices enter a document queue. The original document remains alongside extracted fields and line items, so an operator can check the source, investigate warnings and correct values. Native PDFs and scanned documents use separate extraction paths.

The recording shows 56 documents: 54 ready and two requiring review. Confidence scores help prioritize checking; duplicate warnings or financial validation issues may still prevent automatic readiness.

Finance and purchasing views compare eligible invoice records. Price alerts make changes visible. Purchase-order checks compare supported invoice lines with imported order and goods-receipt records. Proposed payment matches require operator confirmation; unmatched transactions remain available for investigation.

The dashboard is not a live bank balance or profit calculation. This version does not automatically reconcile partial or split payments. The proposed next step is a controlled pilot with real supplier documents, a defined review process and an agreed output format.
