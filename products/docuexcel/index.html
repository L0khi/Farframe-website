Designed for controlled local operation.

DocuForge supports processing and reviewing invoices within your organization’s own environment. Access permissions, backups and data retention remain part of your organization’s operating procedures.

SYSTEM FLOW

From source invoice to usable output.

Processing, review and delivery are separate stages, helping your team check information before using it.

  1. 01

    Add documents

    Add supplier invoices to the local processing queue. Keep source documents available for reference.

  2. 02

    Extract information

    Capture invoice fields, totals, purchase-order references and line-item information.

  3. 03

    Review exceptions

    Check uncertain fields, duplicate warnings and purchasing exceptions against source documents.

  4. 04

    Export records

    Produce structured Excel output for further business review and analysis.

OPERATIONAL APPLICATIONS

Support everyday invoice operations.

FINANCE

Invoice extraction and review

Capture supplier, invoice, tax, currency and total information while retaining access to the original document.

PURCHASING

Line-item price analysis

Compare repeated purchases and surface unit-price changes for commercial review.

CONTROL

Purchase-order checks

Identify invoice lines where cumulative billed quantities may exceed quantities ordered or received.

OUTPUT

Structured Excel delivery

Organize invoice information into consistent columns for review, reporting and further analysis.
